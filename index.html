About Me

Hi, I'm Sania, a BCA student who likes figuring out how things work and then making them work better.

Most of what I build starts with an everyday annoyance: comparing lab test prices across providers, checking the weather without clutter, or finding government scholarships a student actually qualifies for.

I work mostly with HTML, CSS and JavaScript on the front end, and Python (Flask) or Node.js behind it, with SQL and PostgreSQL when the data needs a home. I care about clean code, mobile-first layouts and the small details that make a page feel considered.
